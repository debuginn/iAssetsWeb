FAQ

Before you get started.

What can I do for free?

Manage multi-currency accounts, multiple books and family members. iCloud sync, shared family books and market data require Pro.

How do I use the AI assistant?

Configure a supported model service and API key in Settings, then ask by text or voice. Data edits require confirmation. Check AI responses against your original records.

Is my data sent to AI?

When you use AI, your question and the asset context needed for it are sent to your configured model service. Regular records are stored locally, with optional iCloud features.

How is family management different from sharing?

Family management organizes members and account ownership. Sharing invites others through iCloud to access the same book with assigned permissions.

Are investment quotes real-time?

Multiple stock markets, funds, ETFs, metals and crypto are supported. Coverage, delays and refresh intervals depend on the market and data source.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Built for the Apple ecosystem with Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean. The app is named 知盈 in mainland China and iAssets elsewhere.
